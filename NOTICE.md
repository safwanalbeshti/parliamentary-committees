# Attribution and reuse

Committee transcripts and metadata downloaded by the automation contain
Parliamentary information licensed under the
[Open Parliament Licence v3.0](https://www.parliament.uk/site-information/copyright/open-parliament-licence/).

The plain-English condensations are paraphrases, not official or verbatim records.
Every published session must retain its link to the corresponding official transcript.

The reusable committee-room illustration in `assets/generic-committee-room.svg` is
original project artwork. It does not use Parliamentary video, photography, the Royal
Arms, or the Crowned Portcullis.
