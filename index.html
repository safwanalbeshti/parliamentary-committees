What this is

Parliamentary committees publish full transcripts of their evidence sessions, but they are long and formal. This page retells one session as a short dialogue you can step through at your own pace.

Wording is simplified, exchanges are condensed, and several MPs’ questions may be merged into one voice — nothing here is a direct quote. For the verbatim record, follow the official transcript link: official transcript ↗.
