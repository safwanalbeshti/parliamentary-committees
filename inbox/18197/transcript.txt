Q134 Chair: I want to look first at the position around Northern Shelf cod. Given that there are three distinct sub-stocks, are you content that the ICES advice for a zero TAC—total allowable catch—is a sensible basis for negotiation?

Q135 Chair: No, I am not asking you to. I am asking if you are content that that is a sensible way to negotiate. You have three distinct biological sub-stocks in the northern, the Viking and the southern areas, but they are being treated as one.

Gareth Baynham-Hughes: Our starting point is the science. A zero TAC advice is always extremely difficult, including the risk of closing other fisheries if you cannot have any bycatch, so it is complicated.
