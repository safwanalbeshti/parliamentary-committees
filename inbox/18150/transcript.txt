Q625 Chair: Thank you very much, and welcome. Keir Mather: Thank you, Chair. May I very briefly—I remember your encouragement for me to focus on brevity—thank all the members of the Committee for your continued scrutiny of the HENPS? You are of course right to say, Chair, that the Prime Minister has emphasised the importance of the consultation process and of considering the views of different voices. I think those two things can be true at the same time. Of course, this is a matter for London and it is of enormous consequence to London; at the same time, this is a national question and we have had a range of national answers to it through the consultation.
