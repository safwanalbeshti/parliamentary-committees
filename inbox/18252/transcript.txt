He was as tall as me, he was 6 foot tall. He was a personal trainer; he was well built. If you know anything about mental health units—sorry if this is a bit detailed, but I will try to make it fast—there are three levels: acute, which has maybe 3,500 beds in this country, medium, and then high. There are only regulations on the height of fences in outside areas in medium and high security units. This seems a little bizarre, because if someone is held under the Mental Health Act and is sectioned for their own safety and they want to escape, why would you not keep them safe with a recommended fence height?

Anyway, it was what happened afterward that matters.
